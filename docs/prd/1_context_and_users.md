# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Problem Context
Modern customers engage with businesses across a multitude of channels, including voice, WhatsApp, email, web chat, and social media platforms. Currently, these channels operate in silos. As a result:
- **Customers** are frustrated due to having to repeat themselves across different touchpoints.
- **Support Agents** struggle with context switching and lack a unified view of the customer's journey, leading to slow issue resolution.
- **Businesses** face high operational costs, inconsistent support experiences, and lower customer satisfaction (CSAT) scores.

The business needs an Omnichannel AI Platform that bridges these gaps by understanding intent, predicting urgency, and personalizing responses across all channels in real time.

## Target Users
1. **End Customers**: Individuals reaching out for support or inquiries, expecting fast, accurate, and personalized responses without repeating their history.
2. **Customer Support Agents**: The workforce that needs a unified co-pilot to handle complex queries, augmented by AI for drafting responses and surfacing context.
3. **CX Managers & Supervisors**: Leaders who need macro-level insights into trending intents, agent performance, and automated deflection rates to optimize operations.
