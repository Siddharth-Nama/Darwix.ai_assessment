## Success Metrics

To validate the Omnichannel AI Platform's success, we will track the following Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

1. **Deflection Rate (Target: > 30%)**
   - *Definition*: The percentage of total customer inquiries successfully resolved by the AI without requiring human agent intervention.
   - *Why it matters*: Directly reduces operating costs and queue times.

2. **Average Handle Time (AHT) Reduction (Target: > 20% Decrease)**
   - *Definition*: The average duration of an interaction from the moment a human agent takes over to resolution.
   - *Why it matters*: Measures the effectiveness of the AI Copilot mapping context and drafting responses for the agent.

3. **First Contact Resolution (FCR) (Target: > 85%)**
   - *Definition*: The percentage of issues that are completely resolved in the customer's first interaction, regardless of the channel used.
   - *Why it matters*: A unified context engine should prevent the customer from needing to follow up multiple times.

4. **Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT) (Target: +15 Points)**
   - *Definition*: Survey score taken immediately post-resolution regarding their satisfaction with the support interaction.
   - *Why it matters*: Measures if speed and personalization directly translate into happier customers.
