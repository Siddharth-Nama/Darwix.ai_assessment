## 3. Revenue Potential & ROI Calculation

### Revenue Potential
Beyond pure cost savings, the Omnichannel AI Platform generates revenue through:

1. **Churn Prevention via Next Best Action**
   - The predictive engine identifies customers at high churn risk during live interactions and triggers retention offers (e.g., a discount or a service upgrade).
   - Assumption: 5% of flagged at-risk customers (500 of 10,000/month) are saved with an average LTV of $250 each.
   - **Retention Revenue: $125,000/month per client**

2. **Upsell & Cross-sell at Point of Interaction**
   - When context suggests a positive, engaged customer, the AI Copilot can suggest relevant upgrades or add-ons for the agent to mention.
   - Assumed conversion rate of 2% on 70,000 human-handled chats.
   - Avg. upsell value: $40.
   - **Upsell Revenue: $56,000/month per client**

### ROI Summary
- **Net Benefit (Year 1)**: $4,230,000 − $240,000 = **$3,990,000**
- **ROI**: ($3,990,000 / $240,000) × 100 = **~1,663%**
- **Payback Period**: < **3 weeks** (savings alone cover the investment)
