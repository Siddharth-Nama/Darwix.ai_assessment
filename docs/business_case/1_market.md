# Business Case

## 1. Market Sizing (TAM / SAM)

### Total Addressable Market (TAM)
The global Customer Experience Management (CXM) market was valued at approximately **$13.8 billion in 2024** and is projected to reach **$35.7 billion by 2030**, growing at a CAGR of 16.8%.

Key market drivers:
- Rising customer expectations for real-time, personalized support.
- Rapid adoption of messaging and social channels for commerce.
- Cost pressures pushing enterprises to automate repetitive support tasks with AI.

### Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)
We focus on **mid-to-large enterprises** in the **E-commerce, SaaS, Fintech, and Telecom** sectors with the following characteristics:
- 200+ support agents.
- Processing more than 50,000 customer interactions per month.
- Annual support operations spend of $1M+.

Estimated Addressable Companies in India & South/Southeast Asia: **~40,000 companies**
Average annual contract value (ACV): **~$60,000/year**
SAM: **~$2.4 Billion annually**

### Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) — 3-Year Target
With a focused GTM strategy, a realistic market capture of **1.5%** of SAM in 3 years yields:
- **SOM: ~$36 Million ARR** by Year 3.
